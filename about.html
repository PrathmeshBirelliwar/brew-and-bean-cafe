Our story

Started in the back
of a bicycle shop.

We started with a six-kilo drum roaster in the back of a bicycle shop and a simple idea: coffee tastes better when nobody's rushing it. That hasn't changed. We still roast in small batches, twice a week, and taste every one before it goes on the menu.

Our beans come from three growing regions we've sourced carefully — a washed Ethiopian from Yirgacheffe, a honey-processed Colombian from Huila, and a barrel-aged Sumatran we bring out each winter. Everything is ground to order and pulled to a timer, not a guess.

What started as a small counter in Warje has grown into a neighbourhood spot — a place regulars stop by for their morning cup and stay a while longer than they planned to.
